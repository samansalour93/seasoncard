# Season Card: Marketing Honesty Rules

Short version: **if it would embarrass us in a news story, we don't post it.**
These rules apply to the founder, any helper and any AI agent. No exceptions for "just testing".

---

## The rules

### 1. No fake reviews or testimonials
- Never write, buy or AI-generate a review, quote or "customer story".
- No quotes from friends or family unless they say who they are ("my sister tried it").
- Collect real feedback only. Publish it only with written permission, word for word, no edits to meaning.
- Never offer a reward for a *positive* review. A reward for *any honest* review is okay if it's disclosed.

### 2. No fake before/after
- No edited, filtered or AI-made "before vs after" of a person.
- Illustrated comparisons (a drawn face in two palettes) are okay, **labelled "illustration"** on the slide.
- Real before/after only from a real customer, with permission, unedited apart from cropping.

### 3. No pretending to be a customer. No sockpuppets.
- One brand account per platform. The founder's personal accounts are clearly the founder's.
- Never post "just found this amazing app!" from any account connected to us.
- No second accounts to upvote, comment, answer our own questions or boost numbers.
- No bought followers, likes, views or comments. No engagement pods.

### 4. Always disclose it's ours
- In any community, forum, Reddit thread or comment: say **"I made this"** / **"this is my product"** before or with the link.
- Creators we give a free report or affiliate link must disclose it (`#ad`, "gifted", "affiliate link")
  in the video or on the image itself, not just in the bio.
- Follow each community's rules first. If they ban self-promotion, we don't link at all.

### 5. Frame claims carefully
| ✅ Say | ❌ Never say |
|---|---|
| "measures your undertone, depth, contrast and chroma" | "X% accurate" |
| "consistent: the same photo gives the same result" | "scientifically proven" |
| "shows your closest season and a runner-up" | "your one true season, guaranteed" |
| "your photo stays on your phone" (engine runs in the browser) | "100% private forever" (unless verified) |
| "a starting point: check it in daylight" | "better than a professional analyst" |
| "free result, paid full report" | "free" with a hidden paywall on the result |

- No user counts, "thousands love it", or "#1" claims unless they're real and we can prove them.
- No medical, skin-health or "anti-ageing" claims.
- **Never claim the founder is a painter, artist or colour analyst.** Saman built the app; he is not a painter.
  No "I'm a painter", "made by a painter", "painter tip" or "artist-made", in captions, slides, DMs or bios.
- Celebrity names only as text references ("often typed as"). Never their photos. Never implied endorsement.

### 6. No real people's photos
- No real faces in our content unless it's the founder (their own choice) or a customer who gave written permission.
- Visuals are illustrations and colour swatches.

## Why we are strict

### Platforms ban it
- TikTok bans fake engagement and deceptive use of multiple accounts. Breaking this can get **every associated account** banned
  → [TikTok Integrity & Authenticity](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/integrity-authenticity)
- Reddit bans multiple-account manipulation and asks business owners to be careful how often they post links
  → [Reddit Help: Spam](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043504051-Spam)
- A new brand with zero audience can't survive a ban. The accounts are the business.

### The law
- **US FTC rule (Aug 2024)** bans fake reviews, including AI-generated ones, undisclosed insider reviews,
  reviews bought for a set sentiment, and fake social-media metrics. The FTC can seek civil penalties
  → [FTC press release](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/08/federal-trade-commission-announces-final-rule-banning-fake-reviews-testimonials)
- **FTC influencer guidance:** any free product or payment has to be clearly disclosed (#ad, "sponsored")
  → [FTC Disclosures 101](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers)
- **UK DMCC Act (from 6 Apr 2025):** bans fake and concealed-incentive reviews; fines up to **10% of global turnover**
  → [CMS summary](https://cms.law/en/gbr/legal-updates/no-more-faux-five-stars-the-dmcc-act-bans-fake-reviews)
- We sell worldwide, so we follow the strictest version everywhere.

### The cautionary tale: Medvi
- An AI-run telehealth marketer that the NYT covered in April 2026 as a two-employee company
  → [Drug Discovery & Development](https://www.drugdiscoverytrends.com/the-new-york-times-spotlighted-medvi-the-fda-had-already-warned-the-self-proclaimed-fastest-growing-company-in-history/)
- Reported problems:
  - 5,000+ ads with apparently fabricated doctor personas
  - AI-generated patient testimonials
  - Deepfaked before/after photos
- Consequences:
  - An FDA warning letter (Feb 2026) over misleading claims
  - Several class actions
  → same source, plus [Futurism](https://futurism.com/artificial-intelligence/ai-drug-marketer-medvi-responds)
- The company blamed "an affiliate". **Lesson: we're responsible for what affiliates and creators post in our name.**
- Season Card is also "AI + tiny team". We need to be the opposite story.
