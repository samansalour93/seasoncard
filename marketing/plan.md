**Goal:** 1,000 free scans + 10 paid sales within ~21 days of launch.
**Budget:** close to $0. The costs are time (about 2–3 h/day) and small tools.
**Rules:** everything follows [rules.md](rules.md). Evidence is in [research.md](research.md). Posts are in [posts.md](posts.md).

> **Find your colour season in 30 seconds. Free, and your photo never leaves your phone.**

### Example comments (no link, pure help)

**Someone asks "Am I soft autumn or soft summer?"**
> Those two sit right next to each other. Both are soft and medium. The difference is temperature.
> Quick daylight test: hold something camel and something dusty rose under your chin by a window.
> If camel makes your skin look even and the rose looks a bit chalky, lean Soft Autumn. The reverse, lean Soft Summer.

**Someone says "black makes me look tired"**
> In seasonal colour theory, pure black mostly belongs to the Winter palettes because it's very deep and very high-contrast.
> If you're lower contrast, try charcoal, espresso brown or deep navy. You get the "black" look with less shadow on the face.
> Keeping black away from the face (trousers, shoes) is also an easy fix.

**Someone asks "why do I get different results in different photos?"**
> Mostly lighting. Warm bulbs push skin yellow, shade and phone screens push it blue, and phone cameras
> "correct" white balance on their own. For any analysis (human or app), use indirect daylight,
> no filter, no makeup, and a white wall or sheet behind you.

## 7. The kill rule (decided now, before launch)

> **At about 1,000 completed scans, if we have fewer than 10 Full Report sales, we pivot.**

Details so it can't be fudged:
- **"Completed scan"** = result shown with `quality.ok = true`. Failed scans don't count.
- **Exclude** our own test scans and internal traffic.
- **Report tech-crowd sources (r/SideProject, Product Hunt) separately.** If the target-audience scans alone
  (TikTok/IG/Pinterest/creators) reach 1,000 with <10 sales, the rule triggers.
- **Timing:** apply at 1,000 completed scans or day 28, whichever comes first.
  If we're under 500 scans at day 28, that's a **distribution** failure, not a conversion one. Diagnose that separately.
- Refunds are subtracted from sales.

**Pivot options to consider (decide on the day, not now):**
- Price test: $4.99 report
- Sell the report as a gift / "for two" bundle
- Change what's paid (e.g. free palette, paid capsule wardrobe)
- B2B: white-label the scan for stylists and colour analysts
- Stop the paid product and use the free scan as a lead magnet for something else

## 8. Daily metrics (one row per day in a sheet)

### Product funnel
- Landing visits (by UTM source)
- Scans started
- Scans completed with `quality.ok` → **cumulative total vs 1,000**
- Quality-fail rate and top issue (`low_light`, `color_cast`...) → shows if the lighting education is working
- Report page views
- Checkout started (PayPal opened)
- **Full Report sales** → **cumulative total vs 10**
- Capsule add-on attach rate
- Revenue, refunds
- Season Card shares/downloads (the viral loop)
- Season distribution: if one season dominates, suspect a lighting or engine bias → tell the Engine agent

### Content (per post, check at 24h and 72h)
- Views, **saves**, **shares/sends**, comments, profile visits, link clicks
- Saves + shares per 1,000 views = our main "is this format working" number
- Followers gained

### Outreach and community
- Creator messages sent / replies / yes / posts live / clicks / sales
- Reddit: comments made, removed (if any removal happens, stop and re-read the rules)

### Daily conversion numbers to glance at
- scan→purchase % (the kill-rule number)
- link click→scan started % (landing page health)
